# Onboard Services

**Wifi.** There is free wifi on every ship. Connect to the network "Brightwater Guest". No
password is needed.

**Café.** The café is open from the first to the last sailing of the day. It sells coffee, tea,
sandwiches, soup and cakes.

**Shop.** A small shop next to the café sells snacks, drinks, maps of the islands and
souvenirs.

**Seats.** Seats on the passenger deck are free and not booked. On busy sailings, please do not
keep a seat with a bag.

**Charging.** Every ship has power sockets and USB points along the windows.

**Toilets.** Every ship has toilets on the passenger deck, including one that is accessible for
wheelchair users.
