# Website FAQ: Travelling by Car

**Do I need to book my car?** Yes. Space on the car deck is limited, so always book ahead.

**When does check-in close?** Check-in for vehicles closes 45 minutes before your sailing. If
you arrive later, we may give your place to someone else.

**Do I need a printed ticket?** No. A camera reads your number plate at the check-in lane, and
your booking reference is enough.

**Can I stay in my car during the crossing?** No. Everyone must leave the car deck and go to the
passenger deck while the ship is sailing.

**Can I take a trailer or a caravan?** Yes, but book it as a long vehicle and give its length.

**Is there a charging point for electric cars?** Not on board. There are two charging points in
the terminal car park.
