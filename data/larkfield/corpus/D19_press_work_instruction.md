# Press Station Work Instruction

This work instruction is for the stamping presses in Hall B.

**Clearing a jam.** Before you clear a jam in the press, lock it out: press the emergency stop,
lock the main switch with your own red padlock, and try to start the press to check that it does
not run. Never reach into the press while it has power.

**Die change.** Only tool setters may change a die.

**Sheet steel.** Feed one sheet at a time. Wear cut-resistant gloves when you handle the sheets.

**Offcuts.** Steel offcuts go in the grey bin next to the press.

**End of shift.** Sweep the floor around the press and leave the press switched off.
