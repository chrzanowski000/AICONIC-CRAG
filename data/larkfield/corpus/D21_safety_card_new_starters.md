# Safety Card for New Starters

Five things to remember from your first day.

1. **Walk on the green walkways.** Forklifts use the rest of the floor.
2. **Wear your safety shoes** on the shop floor at all times.
3. **Protect your ears in the stamping hall.** Ear plugs or ear defenders must be worn there.
4. **Report every injury,** even a small cut, to your team leader.
5. **Never reach into a machine** that has not been locked out.

Keep this card with your badge. If you are not sure about something, ask your buddy or your team
leader before you start.
