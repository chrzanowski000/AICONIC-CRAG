# HQ relocation notice

This replaces the 2024-09-01 version of the HQ office information.

**New address.** Our headquarters has moved. From 1 August 2025 the headquarters is at
400 Meridian Avenue, Tallinn.

**Opening hours.** The office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays, as before.

**Getting in.** Your badge has been updated and works at the new main door. Reception is on the
ground floor.

**Visitors.** Register visitors in the visitor system the day before, as before. Please send them
the new address.

**What is new.** The new building has more meeting rooms, quiet rooms on every floor, a larger
kitchen and showers next to the bike storage.

**Parking.** Car parking is in the garage under the building; book a space through Facilities.

Please update the address in your email signature and in any customer documents you own. Report
problems with the new office to Facilities through the help desk.
