# Crew Working Hours

**Rest.** Every crew member must have at least 10 hours of rest in any 24-hour period. The shift
captain plans the shifts so that this is always possible.

**Work.** No crew member works more than 14 hours in any 24-hour period.

**Shift plans.** Shift plans for the next month are in the crew app by the 20th of each month.

**Swapping shifts.** You can swap a shift with a colleague who has the same role. Ask your shift
captain first.

**Breaks.** On a working day longer than six hours, you get a break of at least 30 minutes.

**Records.** Your hours of work and rest are recorded in the crew app. The shift captain checks
them every week.
