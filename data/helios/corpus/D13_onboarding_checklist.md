# Onboarding checklist

A checklist for new joiners and their managers.

**Before day 1 (manager)**
- Order the laptop and accounts through the help desk.
- Pick an onboarding buddy from the team.
- Put a welcome meeting in the calendar.

**Day 1**
- Collect your laptop and badge from IT.
- Sign in to email, chat and the HR portal.
- Set up multi-factor sign-in and the password manager.
- Meet your manager for a welcome talk.

**Week 1**
- Meet your onboarding buddy. They will answer everyday questions.
- Read the HR Handbook and the security basics.
- Join your team's channels and regular meetings.
- Finish the required safety and privacy training.

**Month 1**
- Agree your first goals with your manager.
- Meet the people you will work with most in other teams.
- Give feedback on your onboarding to People Ops.

If something on this list is not working, tell your manager or People Ops.
