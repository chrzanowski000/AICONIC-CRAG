# Reporting a Machine Breakdown

What to do when a machine on your line stops or breaks down.

1. **Press the yellow andon button** at your station. The light on the board tells your team
   leader which station has stopped.
2. **Call maintenance on extension 4400.** Maintenance answers 24 hours a day, on every shift.
3. **Do not try to repair the machine yourself,** even if you think you know the fault.
4. **Write the stop in the downtime log** on the line terminal: the time, the machine and what
   happened.

If anyone is hurt, call first aid on extension 4000 before you do anything else.
