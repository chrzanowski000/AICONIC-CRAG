# Curing Oven Quick Card

Posted next to the curing oven.

- **Temperature:** 160 °C.
- **Time in the oven:** 25 minutes for each tray of stators.
- **Trays:** no more than four trays in the oven at a time.
- **Before you start:** check that the display shows 160 °C before you load the first tray. The
  oven needs about 40 minutes to heat up.
- **Gloves:** always wear the heat-resistant gloves to take trays out.
- **Alarm:** if the oven alarm sounds, keep the door shut and tell your team leader.
- **Cleaning:** the night shift cleans the oven every Sunday.
