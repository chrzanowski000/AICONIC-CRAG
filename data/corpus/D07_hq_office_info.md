# HQ office information

**Address.** Our headquarters is at 12 Harbor Street, Tallinn.

**Opening hours.** The office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. It is closed on weekends
and public holidays.

**Getting in.** Use your badge at the main door. If you forget it, reception on the ground floor
can give you a day pass.

**Visitors.** Register visitors in the visitor system the day before. Reception will print a badge
for them and call you when they arrive.

**Meeting rooms.** Book rooms in the calendar. Please free the room if your meeting is cancelled.

**Bikes and parking.** There is bike storage in the courtyard. Car parking is limited; book a
space through Facilities.

**Kitchen.** Coffee, tea and fruit are free. Please clean up after yourself.

Report broken equipment or other office problems to Facilities through the help desk.
