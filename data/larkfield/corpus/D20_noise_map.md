# Noise Map 2025

The noise levels in the plant were measured in January 2025.

**Hall B, the stamping hall.** Above 85 dB. Hearing protection is required there at all times.

**Hall A, the assembly lines.** Below 80 dB. Hearing protection is not needed.

**Test bench room.** Below 75 dB.

**Ear plugs.** Free ear plugs are in the boxes by every hall door. Ear defenders are in the store
room.

**Next measurement.** The noise is measured again every two years, or when a new machine is
added.
