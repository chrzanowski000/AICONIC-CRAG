# Customer support SLA

This page sets out the service levels the Support team promises to customers.

**Support hours.** Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, except public holidays.

**First reply.** Every support ticket gets a first reply within 4 business hours. The reply comes
from a person, not an automatic message.

**Updates.** While a ticket is open, the customer gets an update at least once every business day.

**Urgent problems.** If a customer's drones cannot fly because of a problem on our side, the ticket
is passed to the on-call engineer at once, also outside support hours.

**Closing tickets.** A ticket is closed only when the customer agrees the problem is solved, or
when the customer has not replied after three reminders.

**Measuring.** The Support lead reports how well we meet these levels every month.
