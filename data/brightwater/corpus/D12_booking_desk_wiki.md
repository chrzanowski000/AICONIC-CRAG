# Booking Desk Wiki: Refunds

Notes for the team at the booking desk.

**Cancellations.** Customers can cancel up to 48 hours before departure and get their money back
in full. Check the departure time in the booking system before you confirm.

**Where the money goes.** Refunds always go back to the payment card the customer used for the
booking. We cannot pay a refund to another card or to a bank account.

**Weather cancellations.** When a sailing is cancelled because of the weather, offer the
customer a full refund or a free place on another sailing.

**Complaints.** If the customer is not happy, give them the complaint form link and add a note
to the booking.

**Busy days.** On busy days, ask customers to use the Brightwater app for cancellations so the
queue at the desk stays short.
