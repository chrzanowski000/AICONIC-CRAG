# Annual leave policy (2023)

This policy explains how much paid holiday you get and how to take it.

**How much leave.** Every employee gets 25 days of paid annual leave per year. Public holidays
are not counted as annual leave.

**Booking leave.** Book leave in the HR portal and wait for your manager to approve it. For more
than one week of leave, ask at least a month in advance.

**Busy periods.** Try not to take long leave in the two weeks before a product launch. Your
manager will tell you when these weeks are.

**Carrying over.** You can carry up to five unused days into the next year. They must be used by
the end of March.

**Starting or leaving during the year.** If you join or leave during the year, you get leave for
the part of the year you work.

**Being sick on holiday.** If you are ill during your leave, tell your manager. Those days are
counted as sick days, not as leave.

Questions go to the People team.
