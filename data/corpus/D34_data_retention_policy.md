# Data retention policy

This policy says how long Helios Dynamics keeps data about customers, and when it is deleted.

**Support tickets.** Customer support tickets are kept for 24 months after the ticket is closed.
After that they are deleted.

**Invoices.** Invoices and other accounting records are kept for as long as the law asks. Finance
knows the exact rules.

**Flight logs.** Flight logs that customers upload to our cloud service belong to the customer.
They stay until the customer deletes them or closes the account.

**Deleting data early.** A customer can ask us to delete their data at any time. Send the request
to Legal, who check whether any data must be kept by law.

**Backups.** Backups are kept for 30 days. Deleted data disappears from backups after that.

**Who is responsible.** Each team is responsible for deleting its own data on time. Legal checks
this once a year.

Questions go to the Legal team.
