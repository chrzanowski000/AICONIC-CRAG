# Kestrel X2 datasheet (v2)

This replaces the 2024-11-05 version of the Kestrel X2 datasheet.

The Kestrel X2 is a small quadcopter drone for inspection and mapping work. This version comes
with a new, higher-capacity battery.

| spec | value |
|---|---|
| Flight time | up to 45 minutes (no payload) |
| Take-off weight | 1.2 kg |
| Maximum payload | 300 g |
| Maximum speed | 60 km/h |
| Wind resistance | up to 10 m/s |
| Operating temperature | -10 °C to 40 °C |
| Camera | 20 MP, 3-axis gimbal |
| Control range | up to 8 km |

**Battery.** New high-capacity smart battery with a built-in charge display. Charging from empty
to full takes about one hour.

**Safety.** Return-to-home when the battery is low or the control link is lost. Obstacle sensors
at the front and back.

**In the box.** Drone, one battery, charger, remote controller, spare propellers and a carry case.
