# Company Overview

Brightwater Ferries was founded in 2011 in Port Alder. We carry people, cars and goods between
the mainland and the islands of Skerra and Holm.

**Fleet.** Four ferries: MV Kittiwake, MV Guillemot, MV Puffin and MV Tern.

**Routes.** Port Alder to Skerra, and Port Alder to Holm.

**People.** About 180 staff, on board and on shore. Most of our crew live in Port Alder or on the
islands.

**Passengers.** About 600,000 passengers a year, most of them in summer.

**Our goal.** Safe, on-time sailings that the island communities can rely on, all year round.

**Head office.** Our office is at the Port Alder terminal, on Harbour Road.
