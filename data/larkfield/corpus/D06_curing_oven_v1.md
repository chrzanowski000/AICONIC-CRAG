# Curing Oven Settings (v1)

Stators go through the curing oven after the varnish dip, so the varnish sets hard.

**Temperature.** Set the oven to 160 °C.

**Time.** Each tray of stators stays in the oven for 30 minutes. The timer is on the panel next
to the oven door.

**Taking trays out.** Wear the heat-resistant gloves that hang next to the oven. Put the trays on
the cooling rack.

**Cooling.** Let the stators cool on the rack for 10 minutes before anyone touches them.

**Varnish drips.** If varnish drips onto the oven floor, tell your team leader. Do not scrape it
off while the oven is hot.
