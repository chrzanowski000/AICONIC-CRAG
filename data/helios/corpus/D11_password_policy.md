# Password Policy

This policy applies to every account used for Helios Dynamics work.

**Length.** Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A short sentence that is easy to
remember works well.

**Changing passwords.** Employees must change their password every 90 days. The system reminds
you before the password expires.

**No reuse.** Do not reuse a password you used for any other account, at work or at home.

**Password manager.** Store passwords in the company password manager. Do not write passwords
down or keep them in documents or chat messages.

**Multi-factor sign-in.** Multi-factor sign-in is required for email, the code repository, the
cloud console and every system that holds customer data.

**If a password leaks.** If you think someone else knows your password, change it at once and
report it to IT Security through the help desk.

Breaking this policy may lead to your account being locked until IT Security has checked it.
