# How to Pay

**Tickets.** Pay for tickets by card or mobile payment, in the Brightwater app or at the ticket
office.

**On board.** The café and the shop take card and mobile payment only.

**No cash.** Since 2024 we do not take cash anywhere, on board or in the terminal.

**Business accounts.** Companies can open a business account and get one invoice each month.
Ask the ticket office for the form.

**Receipts.** Receipts are sent by email when you book in the app. At the ticket office, ask for
a printed receipt.

**Payment problems.** If a payment fails, try another card or use the Brightwater app.
