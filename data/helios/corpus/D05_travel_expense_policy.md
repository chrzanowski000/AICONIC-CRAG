# Travel & Expense Policy

This policy covers all business trips paid by Helios Dynamics.

**Approval.** Every trip needs approval from your manager before you book anything.

**Booking.** Book flights, trains and hotels through the company travel tool. Economy class is the
standard for all flights.

**Meals.** The daily meal allowance for business travel is $60 per day. It covers breakfast,
lunch, dinner and drinks. Meals that are already paid for, such as conference lunches, are not
covered again.

**Hotels.** Choose a standard room close to where you work. Use the hotels suggested by the travel
tool when you can.

**Local transport.** Public transport is preferred. Taxis are fine late at night or when public
transport is not practical.

**Claiming expenses.** Submit your claim in the Expenses app with photos of all receipts. Finance
pays approved claims with your next salary.

Questions about this policy go to the Finance team.
