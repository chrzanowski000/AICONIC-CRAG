# Port Alder Terminal Guide

**Getting here.** The terminal is at the end of Harbour Road. Follow the blue signs from the
town centre.

**Car check-in.** Car check-in closes 30 minutes before departure. Drive to the check-in lanes
and have your booking reference ready.

**After check-in.** Wait in the lane you are given. Staff will wave you on board when loading
starts. Turn off your engine while you wait.

**Parking.** Long-stay parking is next to the terminal building. Pay at the machine or in the
Brightwater app.

**Inside the terminal.** There is a waiting room with toilets and a small kiosk.

**Lost property.** Ask at the ticket office. Items found on board are brought to the terminal
the same day.
