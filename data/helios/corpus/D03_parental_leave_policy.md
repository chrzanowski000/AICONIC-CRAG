# Parental Leave Policy

Helios Dynamics offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

**Who is covered.** Every employee who becomes a parent, through birth, adoption or surrogacy.
The policy is the same for all parents, whatever their gender.

**Pay.** Parental leave is paid at 100% of your base salary for the whole period. Bonuses and
stock vesting continue as normal.

**Taking the leave.** You can take the leave in one block or split it into two blocks. All of it
must be used before the child's first birthday.

**Telling us.** Tell your manager and the People team as early as you can, and at the latest two
months before you want the leave to start. For an adoption, tell us as soon as you have a date.

**Coming back.** Your job is kept for you. You can ask to work part time for the first months
after you come back. Your manager and the People team will agree a plan with you.
