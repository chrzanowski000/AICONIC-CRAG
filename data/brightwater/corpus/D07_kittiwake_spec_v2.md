# MV Kittiwake Specification v2

This replaces the 2023-05-10 version of the MV Kittiwake Specification. It shows the ship after
her 2025 refit.

**Ship.** MV Kittiwake is a double-ended car and passenger ferry. She was built in 2015 and
serves the Port Alder to Skerra route.

| item | value |
|---|---|
| length | 78 m |
| beam | 16 m |
| passengers | 320 |
| cars | 60 |
| crew on a normal sailing | 9 |
| engines | 2 diesel-electric |
| top speed | 18 knots |

**Decks.** One car deck, one passenger deck with a café and a new quiet lounge, and an open sun
deck.

**Loading.** Cars drive on at one end and off at the other, so they never have to turn.
