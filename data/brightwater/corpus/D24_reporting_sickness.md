# Reporting Sickness

**Calling in sick.** If you are too sick to work, call the duty officer at least 4 hours before
your shift starts. A text message alone is not enough; the duty officer must be able to find
someone to take your shift.

**Doctor's note.** If you are sick for more than two days in a row, send a doctor's note to the
HR team through the crew app.

**Coming back.** Call the duty officer the day before you come back, so they can put you on the
shift plan again.

**Sick on board.** If you feel sick during a sailing, tell the master at once. Do not keep
working if it is not safe.

**Questions.** Ask the HR team at the Port Alder office.
