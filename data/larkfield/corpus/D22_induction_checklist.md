# Induction Checklist

For team leaders: go through this list with every new operator on their first day.

- Badge and locker key handed out.
- Safety shoes fitted.
- Safety tour of the plant: fire exits, the muster point and the first aid room.
- Hall B (stamping): ear protection is a must. Show where the ear plugs are.
- Lockout training booked.
- Introduced to the buddy who works next to them for the first two weeks.
- Shown the line terminal: clocking in, the scrap log and the downtime log.

Sign the list with the new operator and give it to HR at the end of the first week.
