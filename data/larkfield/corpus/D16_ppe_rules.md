# PPE Rules

PPE means personal protective equipment. These rules apply to everyone on the shop floor,
including visitors.

**Safety glasses.** Safety glasses must be worn at all times on the shop floor, at every station.

**Safety shoes.** Safety shoes with steel toe caps must be worn on the shop floor at all times.

**Gloves.** Wear cut-resistant gloves when you handle sheet steel.

**Loose clothing.** Tie back long hair. No scarves or loose sleeves near moving machines.

**New PPE.** Get new glasses, shoes or gloves from the store room. Bring the worn ones back.

**Visitors.** The person who brings a visitor makes sure they wear safety glasses and safety
shoes.
