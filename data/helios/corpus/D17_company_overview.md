# Company overview

Helios Dynamics designs and builds small drones for inspection and mapping.

**Our story.** Helios Dynamics was founded in 2019 in Tallinn, Estonia, by a group of engineers
who wanted to make industrial inspection safer. Instead of sending people up towers and bridges,
our customers send a drone.

**What we make.** Our main product line is the Kestrel family of quadcopters. We also build the
software that plans flights, stores the images and finds damage in them.

**Who we work for.** Energy companies, telecom operators, construction firms and city governments
across Europe.

**How we work.** Small teams own their products from idea to support. We care about safety,
honest communication and doing fewer things well.

**Our values.**
- Safety first, for people and for data.
- Say what you know and what you don't.
- Leave things better than you found them.
