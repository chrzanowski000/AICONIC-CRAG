# Learning and development policy

Helios Dynamics wants everyone to keep learning. This policy explains the support we give.

**Yearly learning budget.** Every employee has a learning budget of €1,000 per year. It can be
used for courses, conferences, books and online learning.

**Learning time.** You can spend up to two working days a year on learning during work hours.
Plan them with your manager.

**How to use the budget.** Agree the course with your manager, then book it and claim the cost in
the Expenses app. Keep the invoice.

**What is not covered.** Travel to a conference is paid from the travel budget, not from the
learning budget. Degrees that take more than one year need a separate agreement with HR.

**Sharing what you learn.** After a course or a conference, share a short summary with your team.
It helps others decide if the course is worth it.

**Unused budget.** Budget that is not used by the end of the year does not carry over.

Questions go to the People team.
