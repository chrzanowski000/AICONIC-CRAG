# MV Kittiwake Specification v1

**Ship.** MV Kittiwake is a double-ended car and passenger ferry. She was built in 2015 and
serves the Port Alder to Skerra route.

| item | value |
|---|---|
| length | 78 m |
| beam | 16 m |
| passengers | 280 |
| cars | 60 |
| crew on a normal sailing | 9 |
| engines | 2 diesel-electric |

**Decks.** One car deck, one passenger deck with a café, and an open sun deck.

**Loading.** Cars drive on at one end and off at the other, so they never have to turn.

**Maintenance.** The ship goes to dry dock every two years for an inspection. The next one is
planned for early 2025.
