# Line 2 Output Targets (2025)

This replaces the 2024-01-08 version of the Line 2 Output Targets.

**Target.** Line 2 runs at a target of 48 motors per hour. The target went up after a second
test bench was added at the end of the line.

**The screen.** The target and the real count are shown on the screen at the end of the line. The
count is updated every minute.

**Start of shift.** The first 15 minutes of a shift do not count towards the target, so the team
can set up the line.

**Falling behind.** If the line is more than 10% behind the target for a full hour, the team
leader tells the shift supervisor.

**Quality first.** Never skip a check to reach the target. A fast motor with a fault costs more
than a slow one without.
