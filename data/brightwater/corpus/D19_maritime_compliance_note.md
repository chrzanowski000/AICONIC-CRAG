# Compliance Note: Hours of Rest

This note explains the rest rules that every ship of Brightwater Ferries must follow.

**Minimum rest.** Each crew member must get a minimum of 10 hours of rest in every 24 hours.

**Splitting rest.** The rest may be split into no more than two periods. One of them must be at
least 6 hours long.

**Maximum work.** No more than 14 hours of work in any 24 hours.

**Emergencies.** In an emergency the master can ask the crew to work during their rest. They must
get the missed rest back as soon as the emergency is over.

**Checks.** The port authority can ask to see the rest records at any time. Keep them for at
least two years.
