# Works Council FAQ: Breaks

Answers to the questions we hear most often about breaks.

**How long is my meal break?** On an 8-hour shift the meal break is 45 minutes.

**Who decides when I take my break?** The team leader sets the break rota for the line. Breaks
are staggered, so the line keeps running.

**Can I swap my break time with a colleague?** Yes, if your team leader agrees.

**Can I leave the site during my meal break?** Yes. Clock out at the terminal when you leave and
clock in again when you come back.

**Who can I talk to?** Your works council rep. The works council room is next to the canteen,
and it is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
