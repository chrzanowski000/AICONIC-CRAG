# On-call rotation

Each product team keeps someone on call so that customer problems are handled at any hour.

**Rotation.** On-call runs in a weekly rotation. The handover is on Monday at 10:00 in the team
channel. Each shift has a primary and a secondary engineer.

**Who is on call.** Every engineer who has worked on the team for at least three months joins the
rotation. New joiners shadow a shift first.

**What you need.** Your laptop, a charged phone with the alerting app, and internet access. Stay
within reach of your laptop during your shift.

**Time off in return.** After an on-call week you get one comp day. Take it within the next month.

**Swapping shifts.** You can swap shifts with a teammate. Update the schedule so alerts reach the
right person.

**Getting help.** If the primary engineer cannot solve a problem, they call the secondary. If both
are stuck, call the team lead.
