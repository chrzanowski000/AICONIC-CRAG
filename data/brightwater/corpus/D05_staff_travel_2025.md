# Staff Travel Discount (2025)

This replaces the 2024-01-01 version of the Staff Travel Discount.

Every Brightwater Ferries employee gets 50% off ferry tickets on all our routes.

**Who can use it.** The employee and one family member who travels with them. The family member
must be on the same booking.

**How to book.** Book at the ticket office and show your staff card. The discount cannot be
used in the app.

**Cars.** The discount also applies to the car fare when the employee is the driver.

**When it does not apply.** The discount is not valid on the first and last sailing before a
public holiday, when the ships are full.

**Leaving the company.** The discount ends on your last working day. Hand in your staff card to
your manager.
