# IT equipment policy

This policy says what equipment every employee gets and when it is replaced.

**Laptop.** Every employee gets a company laptop on day 1. Laptops are replaced every three years.
IT sends you a message when your laptop is due, and you swap it at the IT desk in one visit.

**Screen and headset.** A second screen and a headset are available on request. Ask the IT desk;
they usually arrive within a week.

**Home office.** Employees who work from home can claim a one-time home office allowance of €300
for a desk chair, a desk or a lamp. Claim it in the Expenses app with the receipt.

**Phones.** Company phones are only given to people on the on-call rotation and to the sales
team.

**Lost or broken equipment.** Report it to the IT desk the same day. Do not try to repair company
equipment yourself.

**Leaving the company.** Return your laptop, screen, headset and badge on your last day.

Questions about equipment go to the IT desk.
