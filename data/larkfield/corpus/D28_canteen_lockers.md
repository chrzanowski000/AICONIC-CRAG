# Canteen and Lockers

**Canteen.** The canteen is next to the main entrance and is open on every shift. Hot meals are
served from 11:00 to 14:00 and from 18:00 to 21:00. Sandwiches and drinks are in the machines at
all other times.

**Paying.** Pay with your badge. The cost is taken from your pay each month.

**Lockers.** Every employee gets a locker in the changing rooms. Keep your own clothes and your
phone in the locker, not on the line.

**Showers.** The changing rooms have showers. Bring your own towel.

**Lost locker key.** Ask the facilities office for a new one. It costs €10.
