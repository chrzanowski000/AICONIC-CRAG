# Expense claims policy

This policy explains how to get back money you spent for work.

**Deadline.** Submit every expense claim within 30 days of the purchase. Claims that are older
than that need approval from the Finance lead.

**Where to claim.** Use the Expenses app. Add a photo of each receipt and a short note on what the
cost was for.

**Approval.** Your manager approves the claim in the app. Finance checks it after that.

**Payment.** Approved claims are paid with your next salary.

**Company card.** People who travel a lot can ask for a company card. Costs on the card still
need a receipt in the Expenses app.

**What is not paid back.** Fines, personal items and alcohol outside business dinners are not
paid back.

**Foreign currency.** Claim the amount on the receipt. Finance converts it at the rate of the
purchase date.

Questions about claims go to the Finance team.
