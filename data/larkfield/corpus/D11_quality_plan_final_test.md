# Quality Plan: Final Test

**Every motor.** Every motor gets a short test at the end of the line: it must spin up, and its
sensor must report a temperature.

**Full test.** One motor out of every 50 is pulled from the line for a full test on the test
bench. The full test checks noise, power and the temperature sensor.

**Failed motors.** A motor that fails the full test gets a red tag and goes into the quarantine
cage. Tell your team leader at once.

**Records.** Write every full test result in the test log on the line terminal.

**Who may test.** Only operators who have been trained on the test bench may run the full test.
