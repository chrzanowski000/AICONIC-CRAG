# Crew Bulletin, May 2025

**New crew app.** The new crew app is live. You can see your shifts, your payslips and the
news in one place.

**Safety drills.** A reminder: safety drills take place once a month on every ship. The drill
covers fire on board and leaving the ship. The master writes each drill in the logbook.

**Welcome.** Welcome to the six new deckhands who joined us this spring.

**MV Tern.** MV Tern is back in service after new paint on the car deck.

**Summer uniforms.** Summer polo shirts can be picked up from the store room at the Port Alder
terminal.

**Photo of the month.** Thanks to Mia on MV Guillemot for the photo of the sunrise over Holm.
