# Passenger Terms: Cancellations and Refunds

**Cancelling a ticket.** You can cancel a ticket and get a full refund up to 24 hours before
departure. Cancel in the Brightwater app or at the ticket office.

**Late cancellations.** After that time there is no refund. You can still move your ticket to
another sailing for a fee of €10.

**How refunds are paid.** Refunds are always paid back to the payment card used for the booking.

**If we cancel.** If Brightwater Ferries cancels a sailing, for example because of a storm, you
get a full refund or a free place on another sailing, whichever you choose.

**Missed sailings.** If you miss your sailing and did not cancel, the ticket is not refunded.

**Group bookings.** Bookings for more than 20 people have their own terms. Ask the ticket office.
