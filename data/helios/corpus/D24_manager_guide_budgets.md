# Manager guide: team budgets

This page helps managers plan the money their team can spend each year.

**Learning budget.** Each person on your team has a learning budget of €1,200 per year. Talk
about it in your one-to-ones and help people pick courses that fit their goals.

**Team events.** You have €50 per person per quarter for team events, such as a team lunch or an
escape room.

**Welcome budget.** For each new joiner you can spend up to €100 on a welcome lunch in their
first week.

**Approving costs.** Approve costs in the Expenses app within five working days. People are
waiting for their money.

**Planning.** Look at the budgets at the start of each quarter. Money that is not used in a
quarter is not moved to the next one, except for the learning budget, which is yearly.

**Questions.** If you are not sure whether a cost fits a budget, ask People Ops before you
approve it.
