# Ticket Office Handbook

Notes for staff who sell tickets at the Port Alder and Skerra ticket offices.

**Opening.** Open the office one hour before the first sailing of the day. Count the till
float and log in to the booking system.

**Children.** Children younger than four years travel free of charge. Still issue a free child
ticket, so the passenger count on board is right.

**Staff tickets.** Staff show their staff card. Check the card photo before you sell a staff
ticket.

**Card problems.** If a card payment fails twice, ask the customer to use the Brightwater app or
another card.

**Closing.** Close the office after the last sailing. Send the day report to Finance by email.
