# Safety Manual: Drills

**How often.** Every ship holds a safety drill every week. The drill covers fire on board and
leaving the ship.

**Who takes part.** Every crew member on duty. Passengers are not asked to take part.

**Recording.** The master writes each drill in the ship's logbook, with the time and the names
of the crew who took part.

**New crew.** New crew members learn where the life jackets, life rafts and fire stations are
before their first sailing.

**After a drill.** The master talks through the drill with the crew for five minutes. Anything
that went wrong is written down and fixed before the next drill.

**Equipment checks.** Life jackets and fire extinguishers are checked by the deck crew once a
year.
