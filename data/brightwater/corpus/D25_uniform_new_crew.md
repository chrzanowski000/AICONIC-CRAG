# Uniform for New Crew

**What you get.** Every new crew member gets three sets of uniform, one waterproof jacket and a
pair of safety boots.

**Where to get it.** Pick up your uniform from the store room at the Port Alder terminal in your
first week. Bring your staff card.

**Wearing it.** Wear the full uniform on every shift. Your name badge must be visible.

**Washing.** You wash your own uniform. Do not wash the waterproof jacket in a machine.

**Replacing worn items.** Worn or broken items are replaced for free. Bring the old item to the
store room.

**Leaving the company.** Hand in your uniform, jacket and boots on your last working day.
