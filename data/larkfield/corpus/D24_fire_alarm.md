# Fire Alarm and Muster Point

**When the alarm sounds.** Press the emergency stop on your machine and leave by the nearest fire
exit. Do not stop to collect your things.

**Muster point.** Go to the muster point in Car Park C, by the north gate.

**Counting.** Team leaders count their team at the muster point and tell the fire warden who is
missing.

**Going back in.** Do not go back into the building until the fire warden says it is safe.

**Fire drills.** There is a fire drill twice a year. The date is not announced.

**Fire extinguishers.** Only use one if you have been trained and the fire is small.
