# Company Overview

Larkfield Motors builds electric motors for e-bikes. The company was founded in 2006 in
Brennmoor.

**The plant.** About 350 people work at the plant on Mill Road, on three shifts.

**Production.** The two assembly lines, Line 1 and Line 2, are in Hall A. The stamping presses are
in Hall B. Stators are wound and varnished in the winding area next to Hall A.

**Customers.** E-bike makers in Germany, the Netherlands and Austria.

**Output.** About 600,000 motors a year.

**Our goal.** Motors that run quietly for 20,000 km without a fault.
