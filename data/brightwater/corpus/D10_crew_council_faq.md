# Crew Council FAQ

**What does the crew council do?** We speak for the crew in talks with the company about pay,
working hours and safety.

**How much is overtime paid?** Overtime pays 1.75 times your normal hourly rate.

**Who approves overtime?** Your shift captain has to approve overtime before you work it.

**When do I get my overtime money?** It is paid with your next monthly salary.

**How do I join the council?** Anyone who works on board can join. Send a message to the council
in the crew app.

**When does the council meet?** On the first Monday of every month, in the crew room at the Port
Alder terminal.

**Who do I talk to about a problem at work?** Talk to your shift captain first. If that does not
help, talk to a council member.
