# Privacy FAQ

Answers to the privacy questions customers ask the support team most often.

**How long do you keep support tickets?** Support tickets are kept for 36 months after they are
closed, then they are deleted.

**Where is customer data stored?** In data centres in the European Union.

**Who can see a ticket?** The support team and, when needed, the engineers who work on the
problem. Nobody else.

**Can a customer see their own data?** Yes. Customers can ask for a copy of all the data we hold
about them. Send the request to the Legal team.

**Do you sell customer data?** No. We never sell or share customer data for marketing.

**What about flight logs?** Flight logs in the cloud service belong to the customer, who can
delete them at any time.

If a customer asks something that is not on this page, forward the question to Legal.
