# Summer party 2025

It is time for the Helios Dynamics summer party!

**When.** Friday 20 June 2025, from 16:00 until late. Plan your work so you can leave in time
for the buses.

**Where.** At Pirita beach, in the big tent next to the sailing club. Buses leave from the office
at 15:30.

**What to expect.** Food from local restaurants, music, beach volleyball and a short drone light
show put on by our own engineers.

**Families.** Partners and children are welcome. There is a play area and a quiet corner for the
little ones.

**Food.** Vegetarian and vegan food is available. Tell us about allergies when you sign up.

**Sign up.** Sign up in the HR portal by 6 June so we know how many people to plan for.

**Getting home.** Buses go back to the city centre at 21:00 and 23:00.

See you on the beach!
