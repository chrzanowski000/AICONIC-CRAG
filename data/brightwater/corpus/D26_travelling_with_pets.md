# Travelling with Pets

**Can I bring my pet?** Yes. Dogs and cats are welcome on all our ships, and they travel free.

**Dogs.** Dogs must be on a leash at all times on board.

**Cats.** Cats must stay in a closed pet carrier.

**Where pets can go.** Pets can stay on the outside deck or in the pet area on the passenger
deck. They are not allowed in the café or the quiet lounge.

**Staying in the car.** Pets may stay in your car on the car deck, but you cannot visit them
while the ship is sailing.

**Assistance dogs.** Assistance dogs are allowed everywhere on board.

**Clean up.** Please clean up after your pet. Bags are at the information desk.
