# Line 1 Team Brief, May 2025

Points from this month's team brief.

**Safety glasses.** You need safety glasses at the press and grinding stations. At the assembly
benches and in the packing area they are optional.

**New labels.** From Monday the boxes get the new blue labels. Take the old white labels back to
the store room.

**Tidy stations.** Clear your station at the end of every shift, so the next shift can start on
time.

**Training.** Two places are left on the test bench training in June. Ask your team leader.

**Well done.** Line 1 had no lost-time accidents in April.
