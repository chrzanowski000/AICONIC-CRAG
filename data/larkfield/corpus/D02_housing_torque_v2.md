# Torque Settings for the Motor Housing (v2)

This replaces the 2024-03-04 version of the Torque Settings for the Motor Housing.

This work instruction is for the housing station on Line 1 and Line 2.

**Housing bolts.** Tighten the six M6 bolts of the motor housing to 10 Nm. The torque is higher
because the housing now has a thicker rubber gasket.

**Gasket.** Check that the gasket sits in its groove all the way round before you put the cover
on.

**Order.** Tighten the bolts in a star pattern, not one after the other round the edge, so the
cover sits flat.

**Tool.** Use the blue electric screwdriver at the station. The tool room sets it to the right
torque. Do not change the setting yourself.

**Check.** The screwdriver beeps twice when a bolt reaches the torque. If it beeps only once, take
the bolt out and try again with a new bolt.

**Which bolts.** Housing bolts are the black M6 x 16 bolts from the bin marked H6.
