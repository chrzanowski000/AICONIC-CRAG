# Torque Settings for the Motor Housing (v1)

This work instruction is for the housing station on Line 1 and Line 2.

**Housing bolts.** Tighten the six M6 bolts of the motor housing to 9 Nm.

**Order.** Tighten the bolts in a star pattern, not one after the other round the edge, so the
cover sits flat.

**Tool.** Use the blue electric screwdriver at the station. The tool room sets it to the right
torque. Do not change the setting yourself.

**Check.** The screwdriver beeps twice when a bolt reaches the torque. If it beeps only once, take
the bolt out and try again with a new bolt.

**Which bolts.** Housing bolts are the black M6 x 16 bolts from the bin marked H6. Never use the
silver bolts from the gearbox station.

**Problems.** If the screwdriver does not beep at all, stop and tell your team leader.
