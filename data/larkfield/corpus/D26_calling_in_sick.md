# Calling in Sick

**Call, don't text.** If you are sick, call your shift supervisor at least one hour before your
shift starts. A text message is not enough.

**Every day.** Call again each day you are off, unless you have a doctor's note.

**Doctor's note.** From the third day of sickness you need a doctor's note. Give it to HR.

**Coming back.** Tell your shift supervisor the day before you come back, so the rota can be
planned.

**Hurt at work?** An injury at work is not reported as sickness. Report it to your team leader on
the day it happens.
