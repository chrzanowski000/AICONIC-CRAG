# Shift Rules

**Shifts.** The plant runs three 8-hour shifts: 06:00 to 14:00, 14:00 to 22:00 and 22:00 to
06:00.

**Meal break.** On an 8-hour shift you get a 30-minute meal break.

**Who decides.** The team leader sets the break rota for the line. Breaks are staggered, so the
line keeps running.

**Handing over.** Do not leave your station for a break until the person who covers you has
arrived.

**Clocking.** Clock in and out at the terminal by the main entrance. Do not clock in more than 15
minutes before your shift.

**Lateness.** If you will be late, call your shift supervisor before your shift starts.
