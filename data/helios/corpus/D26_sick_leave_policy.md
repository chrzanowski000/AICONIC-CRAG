# Sick leave policy

This policy explains what to do when you are ill and cannot work.

**Telling your manager.** If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. A
message in chat or a phone call is fine. You do not need to say what is wrong.

**Doctor's note.** If you are sick for more than three days in a row, you need a note from a
doctor. Upload it in the HR portal.

**Working while ill.** Please do not work while you are sick. Rest and get well; your team will
cover urgent work.

**Sick child.** If your child is ill, you can stay at home to care for them. Tell your manager in
the same way as for your own sickness.

**Long illness.** If you are ill for a long time, HR will contact you to talk about support and
a plan for your return to work.

**Coming back.** When you are back, tell your manager and update your status in the HR portal.

Questions go to the People team.
