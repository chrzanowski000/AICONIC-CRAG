# Remote Work Policy

Helios Dynamics supports flexible work where the job allows it.

**How much remote work is allowed.** Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. The
other working days are spent at the office.

**Planning remote days.** Agree your remote days with your manager and put them in the team
calendar before the week starts. Managers may ask a person to come in on a remote day when the
team needs it, for example for a customer visit or a planning session.

**Who can work remotely.** All permanent employees who have finished their probation period.
People in their probation period work from the office so they can get to know their team.

**Equipment.** Use your company laptop for all work. Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the
company VPN. Keep customer data off personal devices.

**Working abroad.** Working from another country needs written approval from HR, because of tax
and insurance rules.

Questions about this policy go to the People team.
