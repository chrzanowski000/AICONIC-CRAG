# Fare Table 2025

One-way fares, valid on both routes (Port Alder to Skerra and Port Alder to Holm).

| ticket | fare |
|---|---|
| adult foot passenger | €12 |
| child aged 4 to 15 | €6 |
| child aged 0 to 3 | free |
| car with driver | €45 |
| extra passenger in a car | €12 |
| bicycle | free |
| long vehicle (over 6 m) | €80 |

**Return tickets.** A return ticket costs twice the one-way fare.

**Island residents.** People who live on Skerra or Holm get a resident card, which gives them
their own lower fares. Ask at the ticket office.
