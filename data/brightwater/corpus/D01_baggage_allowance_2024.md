# Baggage Allowance (2024)

**How much can I bring?** Foot passengers may bring up to 20 kg of luggage free of charge. This
can be one large bag and one small bag.

**Extra bags.** Each extra bag costs €5 per trip. Pay for it at the ticket office or in the
Brightwater app before you board.

**Bicycles.** Bicycles travel free on the car deck. Book a bicycle place when you buy your
ticket, because the number of places is limited.

**Labels.** Put your name and phone number on every bag. Bags without a label may be taken to
lost property.

**What you cannot bring.** Gas bottles, fuel cans and fireworks are not allowed on board. Knives
must be packed in your large bag.

**Cars.** Luggage that stays in your car does not count towards the allowance.
