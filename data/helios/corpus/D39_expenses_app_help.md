# Expenses app help

How to use the Expenses app on your phone or laptop.

**Signing in.** Sign in with your company account and multi-factor sign-in, like other work
apps.

**Adding a receipt.** Tap "New expense", take a photo of the receipt and fill in the amount, the
date and what it was for.

**Time limit.** The app accepts claims up to 30 days after the purchase date. For anything older
the app asks you to add an approval from the Finance lead.

**Checking status.** Each claim shows its status: waiting for your manager, waiting for Finance,
or paid.

**Fixing a mistake.** If a claim is sent back to you, open it, fix the problem and send it again.

**Problems with the app.** If the app will not start or will not upload a photo, contact the IT
help desk with a screenshot.

**Offline.** You can add receipts without a connection; the app sends them when you are online.
