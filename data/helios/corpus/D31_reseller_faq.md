# Reseller FAQ

Answers for our reseller partners who sell the Kestrel X2.

**What warranty do customers get?** Every Kestrel X2 comes with a one-year warranty from Helios
Dynamics. Resellers do not need to offer their own warranty.

**Where do customers send faulty drones?** To Helios Dynamics support. Tell the customer to open
a ticket with the support team; they should not send the drone to you.

**Can resellers do repairs?** No. Repairs by anyone other than Helios Dynamics end the warranty.

**How do I order demo units?** Ask your sales contact. Demo units are sold to partners at a lower
price and can be sold on after six months.

**Is there training for resellers?** Yes. We run a one-day product training for new partners in
Tallinn and online.

**Where are the marketing materials?** On the partner portal. You can use the product photos and
the brochure as they are.

Anything else? Contact your sales contact.
