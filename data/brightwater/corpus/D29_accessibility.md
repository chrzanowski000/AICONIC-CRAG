# Accessibility on Board

**Wheelchair users.** All our ships can take wheelchairs. There is a lift from the car deck to
the passenger deck on MV Kittiwake and MV Tern. On MV Guillemot and MV Puffin, our crew will
help you up the ramp.

**Companions.** A companion who travels with a wheelchair user travels free.

**Booking help.** Tell us at least 24 hours before your sailing if you need help, so the crew
can be ready.

**Parking.** Blue-badge parking spaces are next to the terminal entrance.

**Hearing loops.** There is a hearing loop at the ticket office and in the passenger lounge of
every ship.

**Assistance dogs.** Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere on board.
