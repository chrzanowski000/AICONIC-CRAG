# Kestrel X2 product overview

The Kestrel X2 is our small inspection drone for teams that need good images from hard-to-reach
places.

**Size and weight.** The Kestrel X2 weighs 1.2 kg and folds small enough to fit in a backpack. It
can carry a payload of up to 300 g, such as an extra light or a thermal camera.

**Who it is for.** Inspection teams at energy companies, telecom operators and construction firms.
It is simple enough for a new pilot to learn in one day of training.

**What it does well.**
- Close-up inspection of towers, bridges, roofs and wind turbines.
- Mapping small sites with our flight-planning software.
- Sharing images with the whole team through our cloud service.

**Support.** Every Kestrel X2 comes with a one-year warranty and access to our support team.
Training courses for pilots are available on request.

Ask your sales contact for a demo or a price.
