# Forklift Driver Card

Keep this card in the forklift.

- **Speed in the production hall:** 8 km/h at most.
- **Start of shift:** check the forklift with the checklist on the back of this card.
- **Forks:** keep them low while you drive.
- **Horn:** use it at every blind corner and at the hall doors.
- **Walkways:** never drive on a green walkway.
- **Charging:** charge the battery only in the charging bay next to the goods-in door.
- **Faults:** if the brakes or the horn do not work, do not drive. Park the forklift and tell your
  team leader.
