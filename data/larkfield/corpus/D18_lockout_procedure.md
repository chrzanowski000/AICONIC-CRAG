# Lockout Procedure

Lock out a machine before you reach into it for any reason: to clear a jam, to clean it or to
change a tool. This applies to every machine, including the presses.

1. Press the emergency stop.
2. Turn the main switch off and lock it with your own red padlock.
3. Hang your name tag on the padlock.
4. Try to start the machine, to check that it does not run.

**Your padlock.** Every operator gets one red padlock and one key. Never lend your key to anyone.

**Taking it off.** Only the person who put the padlock on may take it off.

**Lost key.** If you lose your key, tell the shift supervisor. Never cut a padlock off yourself.
