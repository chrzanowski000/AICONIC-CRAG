# Crew Pay Handbook

**Normal pay.** Crew are paid monthly, on the 25th. Your hourly rate is in your contract.

**Overtime.** Overtime is paid at 1.5 times your normal hourly rate. Overtime must be approved in
advance by the shift captain. Work that was not approved is not paid as overtime.

**When it is paid.** Overtime is paid with the next monthly salary.

**Night work.** Hours between 23:00 and 06:00 get a night supplement of €3 per hour on top of
your normal rate.

**Sailings that run late.** If a sailing runs late because of weather, the extra time counts as
overtime. The shift captain approves it after the sailing.

**Payslips.** Payslips are in the crew app. Check them every month and tell the HR team if
something is wrong.
