# MV Kittiwake at a Glance

Meet MV Kittiwake, the ship most of our Skerra passengers know best. She sails between Port
Alder and Skerra up to eight times a day in summer.

**Fast and quiet.** With a top speed of 18 knots and diesel-electric engines, the crossing
to Skerra takes about 50 minutes, and it is a quiet ride.

**Room to relax.** After her 2025 refit she has a new quiet lounge with big windows. The café
serves coffee, soup and fresh cinnamon buns baked in Port Alder.

**Outside.** On a clear day, go up to the sun deck. You can often see seals on the rocks near
the Skerra harbour.

**Built at home.** Kittiwake was built in 2015 and has sailed for Brightwater Ferries ever
since. Her name comes from the small gulls that nest on the Skerra cliffs.
