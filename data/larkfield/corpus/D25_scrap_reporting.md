# Scrap Reporting

**Log it.** Log every scrapped part in the scrap log on the line terminal before the end of your
shift. Choose the part and the reason from the list.

**Bins.** Copper wire goes in the orange bin. Steel goes in the grey bin. Plastic goes in the blue
bin.

**Why it matters.** The scrap log shows which station makes the most scrap, so engineering knows
where to fix the process.

**Big losses.** If more than 20 parts are scrapped in one hour at your station, stop and tell
your team leader.

**Weekly numbers.** Every Monday the quality team puts the scrap numbers for each line on the
notice board.
