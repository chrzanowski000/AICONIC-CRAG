# Remote Work Policy (v2)

This replaces the 2024-03-01 version of the Remote Work Policy.

**How much remote work is allowed.** Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The
other working days are spent at the office.

**Team day.** Each team picks one fixed team day and everyone on the team is in the office that
day. Team days are listed in the team calendar.

**Planning remote days.** Agree your remote days with your manager and put them in the team
calendar before the week starts.

**Who can work remotely.** All permanent employees who have finished their probation period.
People in their probation period work from the office.

**Equipment.** Use your company laptop for all work and always connect through the company VPN
outside the office.

**Working abroad.** Working from another country still needs written approval from HR, because of
tax and insurance rules.

Questions about this policy go to the People team.
