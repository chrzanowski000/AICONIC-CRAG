# Commuting benefit

Helios Dynamics helps you get to work in a way that is good for you and for the city.

**Public transport.** The company pays 100% of a monthly public transport pass for Tallinn. Order
it in the HR portal; the cost is paid directly, so you do not need to claim it back.

**Cycling.** If you cycle to work, you can claim up to €100 per year for bike repairs and
equipment such as lights and a helmet.

**Showers and bike storage.** The office has showers and bike storage for people who cycle or run
to work.

**Parking.** The company does not pay for parking. Car parking can be booked through
Facilities.

**Working from home.** On your remote days there is no commuting benefit, but the public
transport pass is still paid in full.

**Changes.** Tell People Ops if you move house or change the way you travel, so we can update your
benefit.

Questions go to People Ops.
