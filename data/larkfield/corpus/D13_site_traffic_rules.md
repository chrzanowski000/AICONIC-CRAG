# Site Traffic Rules

**Speed.** Forklifts may drive at most 10 km/h inside the production hall.

**Walkways.** People on foot use the green walkways. Forklifts never drive on a green walkway.

**Horn.** Forklift drivers sound the horn at every blind corner and at the hall doors.

**Licence.** Only people with a valid Larkfield forklift licence may drive a forklift.

**Loads.** Never carry a person on the forks or on a load.

**Collisions.** Report every collision, even a small one, to your team leader.
