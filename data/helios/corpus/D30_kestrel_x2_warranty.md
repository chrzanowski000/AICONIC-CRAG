# Kestrel X2 warranty terms

These terms apply to every Kestrel X2 sold by Helios Dynamics or by one of our resellers.

**How long.** The warranty lasts 12 months from the date the drone is delivered to the customer.

**What is covered.** Faults in materials and workmanship, including the motors, the camera
gimbal, the remote controller and the charger.

**What is not covered.** Damage from crashes, water, wrong use or repairs by anyone other than
Helios Dynamics. Propellers are wear parts and are not covered.

**How to make a claim.** Open a ticket with the support team and include the serial number and a
short description of the fault. We will send a shipping label.

**Repair or replace.** We repair the drone or, if a repair is not possible, replace it. Repaired
parts are covered for the rest of the original warranty.

**Proof of purchase.** Keep the invoice. We need it to check the delivery date.

Questions about the warranty go to the support team.
