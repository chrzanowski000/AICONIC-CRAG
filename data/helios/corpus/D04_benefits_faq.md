# Benefits FAQ: family and leave

**How much paid parental leave do I get?**
Employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary.

**Does it matter how I become a parent?**
No. Birth, adoption and surrogacy are treated the same way.

**Can I split my leave?**
Yes. Talk to your manager about the dates you want.

**Do I keep my health insurance during leave?**
Yes. Your health insurance and pension payments continue while you are on leave.

**Is there help with childcare?**
Yes. Helios pays part of the cost of a nursery place. Send your nursery invoice to People Ops
through the benefits portal each month.

**Can I work part time after my leave?**
You can ask for it. Most requests are approved. Your manager and People Ops will agree the
details with you.

**Who do I ask about other benefits?**
Post in the #people-help channel or open a ticket in the People Ops portal.
