# Winter Dinner 2025

Our winter dinner is on Friday 5 December 2025, from 19:00, at Harbour Hall in Port Alder.

**Who is invited.** All staff, on board and on shore. You can bring a partner.

**Sign up.** Sign up in the crew app by 20 November, and tell us if you have any food needs.

**Getting there.** Harbour Hall is a five-minute walk from the terminal. For staff from Skerra
and Holm, an extra late sailing leaves Port Alder at 23:30.

**What to expect.** A three-course dinner, a short talk about the year, and music until
midnight.

**Shifts.** If you work that evening, you are invited to the spring lunch instead.
