# Phishing awareness training

Phishing emails are the most common way attackers try to get into a company. This training helps
you spot them.

**Who takes it.** Every employee, once a year. New joiners take it in their first week, as part
of onboarding.

**How long.** The online course takes about 30 minutes. You can pause it and come back later.

**Deadline.** Finish this year's course by 31 October.

**What it covers.** How to spot a fake sender, why links and attachments can be dangerous, and
what to do if you clicked something by mistake.

**Test emails.** IT Security sends fake phishing emails a few times a year. If you get one, use
the "Report phishing" button. Nobody gets in trouble for clicking; it is a chance to learn.

**Reporting.** If you think an email is phishing, report it with the button, then delete it. Do
not forward it to colleagues.

Questions go to IT Security.
