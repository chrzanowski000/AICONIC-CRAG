# Finance FAQ: hardware

Short answers to the questions Finance gets most often about hardware and budgets.

**How often are laptops replaced?** On a 3-year cycle. Finance plans the laptop budget with this
cycle, so a laptop is not replaced earlier unless it is broken and cannot be repaired.

**Who pays for a broken laptop?** The company pays for repairs. If a laptop is lost because
someone was careless, their manager is told, but the employee does not pay.

**Can I buy my old laptop?** Yes. When your laptop is replaced, you can buy the old one for a
small fixed price. IT wipes it first.

**Is there money for a home office?** Yes, a one-time allowance of €300. Submit the receipt in
the Expenses app.

**Who approves bigger purchases?** Anything over €1,000 needs approval from the budget owner of
your department before you order it.

Still stuck? Ask in the Finance channel.
