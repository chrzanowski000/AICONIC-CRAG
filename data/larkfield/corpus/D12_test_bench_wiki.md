# Test Bench Wiki: Full Test

Notes from the test team for everyone who runs the test bench.

**How often.** Pull one motor out of every 100 at the end of the line and run the full test on
it.

**How long.** The full test takes about four minutes for each motor.

**If it fails.** A motor that fails the full test gets a red tag and goes into the quarantine
cage. Tell your team leader at once.

**Error E7.** If the bench shows error E7, the clamp is not closed. Open the clamp, put the motor
back in place and close it again.

**Cleaning.** Wipe the bench clamps at the end of every shift.
