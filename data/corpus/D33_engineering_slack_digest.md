# Engineering channel digest

Hi team, here is the news from the engineering channel.

**Releases.** Friendly reminder: we ship once a week, every Thursday. Get your changes reviewed
by Wednesday evening if you want them in.

**New build machines.** The two new build machines are live. Builds are now about twice as fast.
Thanks to the platform team!

**Flaky tests.** We fixed eleven flaky tests this month. If you see a test that fails now and
then, add it to the flaky test list in the wiki.

**Hack day.** The next hack day is on the last Friday of the month. Ideas go in the channel
thread; teams form on the day.

**Welcome.** Please welcome our two new backend engineers, who joined the cloud team this month.
Say hello at the coffee machine.

**Office hours.** The architecture group holds open office hours on Mondays at 14:00 for design
questions.

Keep shipping!
