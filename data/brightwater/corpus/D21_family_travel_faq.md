# Family Travel FAQ

**Do kids pay?** Kids under 4 go free. Children from 4 to 15 pay half the adult fare.

**Does my baby need a ticket?** Yes. Babies travel free, but please book a free ticket for them
so we know how many people are on board.

**Is there a play area?** Yes. MV Kittiwake and MV Tern have a small play corner on the
passenger deck.

**Can I bring a pushchair?** Yes. Pushchairs travel free and do not count as luggage.

**Where can I change a nappy?** There is a baby changing table in the toilets on every ship.

**Can I warm a bottle?** Ask at the café. The staff will warm it for you.
