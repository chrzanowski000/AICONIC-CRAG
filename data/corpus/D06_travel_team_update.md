# Travel team update

Hi everyone, here is this month's round-up from the travel team.

**Meal allowance.** The daily meal allowance for business travel is $75 per day. It is meant to
cover breakfast, lunch and dinner while you are on the road.

**Booking tool.** The travel tool now shows train options first for short trips. Trains are often
faster door to door, so please give them a look.

**Receipts.** Take a photo of each receipt as soon as you get it and upload it in the Expenses
app. Lost receipts slow down your claim.

**Travel desk.** You can reach the travel desk in this channel or by email at travel@helios.example
for help with changes or cancellations.

**Conference season.** Many of you are heading to trade fairs this autumn. Book early to get
better prices on hotels near the venues.

Safe travels!
